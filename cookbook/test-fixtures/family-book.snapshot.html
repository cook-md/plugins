Tomato Soup

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 30 min

Source: Grandma

Per serving: 180 kcal · 4 g protein · 20 g carbs · 9 g fat

Ingredients

  • 1 onion (diced)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 800 g tomatoes
  • salt

Cookware

  • pot

Method

A family favourite.

1

Chop 1 onion and 2 cloves garlic.

2

Fry in pot with 2 tbsp olive oil, add 800 g tomatoes and simmer for 25 minutes.

Finish

1

Blend with salt until smooth.
